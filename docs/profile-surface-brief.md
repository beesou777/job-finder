# Career profile workspace

## Scope

Operate mode. Primary route: `/dashboard/profile`. Audience: job seekers maintaining accurate career information for KamKhoj's future application workflow. The user asked to see a mockup, then instructed implementation to continue. Preserve the real wordmark, existing dashboard navigation, and actual backend behavior. The generated sample data, decorative logo and tagline are not shipping content.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Give users a clear, editable source of career facts, separating drafts from confirmed information and from application authorization.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit KamKhoj's white surfaces, light-blue workspace, navy headings, blue actions, existing font and modest rounded controls. No rebrand or invented statistics.

STORY: Enter personal details, review experience and education, add skills/projects/links, choose AI preferences, then save a draft or explicitly confirm facts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Profile heading and copy-download action above four numbered stages. A wide editing surface sits beside a narrower “Before you apply” guide. On smaller screens, the guide follows the form and stages form a two-column grid.

FORM: User-approved mockup direction translated into the incumbent dashboard. No concept seed: the composition was selected through the supplied preview. The defining interaction is explicit CV-suggestion review with confirmation reset after fact edits; use restrained color transitions rather than decorative motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Deferred verification

The user explicitly deferred checks until the final stage. Browser captures, accessibility checks, design detector, build, typecheck and test execution are pending. Do not label this surface production-ready. No generated raster is referenced by the running application.
