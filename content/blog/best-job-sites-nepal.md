## Individual Portal Comparison

### MeroJob

**Best for:** IT jobs, corporate positions, fresh graduates

**Pros:** Large database, user-friendly interface, active listings

**Cons:** Requires account creation for some features

### JobsNepal

**Best for:** Established professionals, banking sector

**Pros:** Long-standing reputation, quality listings

**Cons:** Smaller database compared to newer portals

### KumariJob

**Best for:** Government jobs, banking, finance

**Pros:** Strong in traditional sectors

**Cons:** Less focus on tech startups
