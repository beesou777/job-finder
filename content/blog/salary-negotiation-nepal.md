## Introduction

Salary negotiation can be uncomfortable, but it's an important skill in Nepal's job market. This guide will help you negotiate confidently and fairly.

## Research Market Rates

### Before Negotiating
- Research average salaries for your role in Nepal
- Check industry standards
- Consider your experience level
- Factor in location (Kathmandu vs. other cities)

### Resources
- Job portals (JobKhoj, MeroJob)
- Industry reports
- Professional networks
- Salary surveys

## When to Negotiate

### Best Times
- After receiving a job offer
- During annual reviews
- When taking on additional responsibilities
- When you have a competing offer

### Avoid Negotiating
- During the first interview
- If you're desperate for the job
- Without market research
- In an unprofessional manner

## How to Negotiate

### 1. Be Prepared
- Know your worth
- Have a target range in mind
- Prepare your talking points
- Practice your pitch

### 2. Be Professional
- Express gratitude for the offer
- State your case clearly
- Provide justification (experience, skills, market rates)
- Be respectful and polite

### 3. Consider the Full Package
- Base salary
- Benefits (health insurance, provident fund)
- Bonuses
- Professional development opportunities
- Work-life balance

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Initial Offer is Low
- Thank them for the offer
- Present market research
- Propose a reasonable counter-offer
- Be willing to compromise

### Scenario 2: They Say No
- Ask about future salary reviews
- Negotiate other benefits
- Consider if the role is still worth it
- Make an informed decision

### Scenario 3: Multiple Offers
- Compare total compensation packages
- Consider growth opportunities
- Think about company culture
- Choose what aligns with your goals

## Tips for Success

- Be confident but not arrogant
- Focus on value you bring
- Be flexible and open to alternatives
- Know when to walk away
- Get everything in writing

## Conclusion

Salary negotiation is a skill that improves with practice. Do your research, be professional, and know your worth. [Explore job opportunities](/jobs) on JobKhoj to find positions that match your expectations.
