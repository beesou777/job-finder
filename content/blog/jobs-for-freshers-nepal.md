## Introduction

Starting your career as a fresher in Nepal can be challenging, but there are plenty of opportunities if you know where to look. This guide will help you navigate the job market as a new graduate.

## Best Industries for Freshers

### 1. IT and Software Development

The tech industry in Nepal is growing rapidly and actively hires freshers. Many companies offer training programs for new graduates.

### 2. Banking and Finance

Banks regularly hire fresh graduates for entry-level positions. These roles often include comprehensive training programs.

### 3. Marketing and Digital Media

With the growth of digital marketing, there are increasing opportunities for freshers in this field.

## Tips for Freshers

- Highlight your education and any internships
- Emphasize soft skills and willingness to learn
- Consider internships as a stepping stone
- Build a portfolio or GitHub profile
- Network with professionals in your field
