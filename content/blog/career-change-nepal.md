## Introduction

Changing careers can be challenging but rewarding. This guide helps you navigate a career change in Nepal's job market.

## Why Change Careers?

### Common Reasons
- Better opportunities
- Higher salary
- Work-life balance
- Interest in new field
- Industry decline
- Personal growth

## Steps to Career Change

### 1. Self-Assessment
- Identify your skills
- Determine interests
- Assess values
- Consider lifestyle goals

### 2. Research New Field
- Industry trends
- Required skills
- Salary expectations
- Job market demand

### 3. Skill Development
- Take courses
- Get certifications
- Build portfolio
- Gain experience (internships, projects)

### 4. Network
- Connect with professionals
- Attend industry events
- Join online communities
- Seek mentors

### 5. Make the Transition
- Update resume
- Highlight transferable skills
- Apply strategically
- Be patient

## Transferable Skills

Skills that work across industries:
- Communication
- Problem-solving
- Leadership
- Project management
- Customer service
- Time management

## Common Career Transitions

### IT to Other Fields
- Project management
- Business analysis
- Consulting
- Teaching

### Banking to Other Fields
- Finance roles
- Consulting
- Real estate
- Entrepreneurship

### Teaching to Other Fields
- Training and development
- Content creation
- Educational technology
- Corporate training

## Challenges

- Starting at entry level
- Salary adjustment
- Learning curve
- Age considerations
- Family support

## Tips for Success

- Be realistic about timeline
- Financial planning
- Start with side projects
- Network actively
- Stay positive
