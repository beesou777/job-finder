## Introduction

A well-written resume is your first impression on employers in Nepal. This guide covers everything you need to know about creating a resume that stands out in the Nepali job market.

## Essential Resume Sections

### 1. Personal Information
- Full name (as per citizenship)
- Contact details (phone, email)
- Location (city, Nepal)
- LinkedIn profile (if available)

### 2. Professional Summary
Write a compelling 2-3 sentence summary highlighting your key skills and experience. This is your elevator pitch.

### 3. Work Experience
List your work history in reverse chronological order:
- Company name
- Job title
- Duration
- Key achievements (use bullet points)

### 4. Education
Include:
- Degree name
- University/College
- Graduation year
- Relevant coursework (if applicable)

### 5. Skills
List both technical and soft skills relevant to the job you're applying for.

## Nepal-Specific Tips

### Language Considerations
- Most corporate jobs prefer English resumes
- Government jobs may prefer Nepali
- Include both if applying to diverse companies

### Format Preferences
- Keep it to 1-2 pages maximum
- Use clear, professional fonts
- Include a professional photo (optional but common in Nepal)

### Key Information to Include
- Citizenship number (if required)
- Work permit status (for non-Nepali citizens)
- Language proficiency (Nepali, English, Hindi)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Spelling and grammar errors
- Too much information (keep it concise)
- Missing contact information
- Outdated information
- Generic resume (tailor for each job)

## Resume Templates

You can find free resume templates online or use professional services. Make sure the template is:
- Clean and professional
- Easy to read
- ATS-friendly (if applying online)

## Conclusion

A great resume opens doors. Take time to craft it well, and don't forget to tailor it for each job application. [Browse jobs](/jobs) on JobKhoj to find opportunities that match your skills.
